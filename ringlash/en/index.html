Ringlash Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026

This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy, provided for convenience. If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

This privacy policy explains what information Ringlash (the "app") handles and how. The developer is YEONGUK KIM (the "developer"). The app has no sign-up or sign-in and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. The developer does not run a server for the app.

1. Information we handle

Information kept only on your device

  • Game progress: your stage, gold, upgrades, rebirth stones, check-ins and achievements, settings, and which purchased features you own are stored on your device. They are not sent to the developer and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Purchase processing records: so that one purchase is not applied twice, the transaction number and product reported by the store are recorded only on your device.

Information you export yourself

  • Backup file: created only when you make a backup file in Settings, and it contains your game progress. The file goes wherever you choose through your device's share sheet. The developer does not receive it. Wherever you keep the file, such as cloud storage or email, that service's policy applies.

Information handled by outside services

  • Crash reports: if the app closes unexpectedly or an error occurs, Firebase Crashlytics sends where the error happened and the app state at that moment, device information (model, operating system and version, CPU type, memory and storage, screen orientation, jailbreak status and similar), app information (version, and whether the app was on screen), and an identifier created per installation. No name, account, or other user identifier is attached.
  • In-app purchases: payments are handled by the App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. The developer does not receive payment details such as card numbers, and does not keep purchase records on a server.

The app shows no ads and does not collect usage analytics.

2. Purposes

  • Fixing errors and improving app stability
  • Providing purchased features and restoring purchases

5. Security measures

  • Crash reports are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Game progress is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not collect information that identifies a person. For residents of the Republic of Korea, children under 14 may not use the app without the consent of a legal guardian. Please contact us at the address below with any questions.

7. Sale of personal information

The developer does not sell the information it handles to third parties.
