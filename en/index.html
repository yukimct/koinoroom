DogPuzzle Privacy Policy

Effective: August 10, 2026 · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy, provided for convenience. If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

This privacy policy explains what information DogPuzzle (the "app") handles and how. The developer is YEONGUK KIM (the "developer"). The app has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

1. Information we handle

Information handled when you use the app

  • Anonymous account identifier: created at random the first time the app runs, so you can take part in rankings and Play together. It contains nothing that identifies you personally.
  • Scores and ranking records: today's score, total score, daily ranking records, and records of ranking rewards, event rewards, and gifts being given and claimed are stored on our server.
  • Board records (from app 1.5.0): for each board in the Daily Puzzle Time Trial, Number Puzzle, and The Walk event, we store the score, time taken, mistakes, use of tools, ads, and coins, device type, and app version, and delete them after one year. For the Time Trial we also store when each dog was placed; top records show those times as a bar to other users together with the nickname. To detect abuse, we may attach a suspicion flag or exclude a record from rankings.
  • Mailbox records (from app 1.4.5): the list of notices shown in the Mailbox, read and cleared marks, and when you opened the Mailbox. The notice list and marks are deleted after 30 days.
  • Gameplay records: app launches, cleared level numbers, tutorial progress, ad views, coins earned and spent, and purchase types, together with the device type (iOS or Android) and the app version. We use them to improve the service and to detect abuse. Each account's highest level is also stored.
  • Coin balance: to detect abuse, the balance and the time of your last visit are recorded on the server. The original balance lives on your device; the server record is a reference copy.

Information handled only if you choose

  • Nickname and profile dog: stored only if you set them to appear in rankings. They are shown on ranking screens and to other users in the same Play together room.
  • Play together (versus and co-op) records: your placement, time, and wins or losses in games you join. They are shown with your nickname to users in the same room.
  • Reports: if you report another user in Play together, we store the reporter, the reported user, the reason, and the room number.
  • Device transfer save: only if you use device transfer, your game progress (level, coins, records) is kept on the server for a short time. It is deleted automatically after 7 days if unused, or within a day after use.
  • Promotional alerts (from app 1.4.2): only if you turn on Promotional alerts, we store the device token for sending notifications, the device type, the app language, the app version, and the date and time you agreed. See section 3.
  • Notification open records (from app 1.4.2): when a notification opens the app, we record which notification it was, the device type, and the time. We do not record who opened it. This is used only to see how notifications are read.
  • In-app purchase records: payments are processed by the App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. The developer never receives payment details such as card numbers. The server records the product, the displayed price and currency, the store, the order number, and the coins received. If you buy the support item, a supporter mark is shown in rankings.

Information handled by third-party services

  • Advertising identifier and approximate location: processed by Google AdMob to show ads. Personalized ads are used only if you agree to ad consent and, on iOS, allow app tracking. You can use every feature of the app without agreeing. The developer does not store location information.
  • Crash reports (from app 1.4.2): if the app closes unexpectedly, Firebase Crashlytics sends where the error happened, device information (model, operating system, memory and storage, screen orientation), and an identifier created per installation. No nickname or account identifier is attached.
  • Identifier for sending notifications (from app 1.4.2): Firebase Cloud Messaging processes an installation identifier to deliver notifications.

The originals of your game progress, such as level, coins, the board in progress, and settings, are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

2. Purposes

  • Providing features such as rankings, Play together, and device transfer
  • Paying ranking rewards and detecting abuse
  • Showing ads. Personalized ads only with your consent
  • Sending promotional alerts. Only with your consent. If you turn them on, we also send service notices such as Mailbox rewards arriving or about to expire
  • Fixing errors and improving the service

3. Promotional alerts

  • We send news about events and offers as notifications. Receiving them is optional and they are off by default. You can use every feature of the app without them.
  • Each notification title starts with an advertising mark, and the body ends with how to opt out.
  • With alerts on, you also receive service notices, such as gifts or ranking rewards arriving and claim deadlines, through the same device token. Service notices are not advertising and carry no advertising mark. Turning alerts off stops them too.
  • We do not send them between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. Korea Standard Time.
  • When you turn them on or off in the app, we show the result with the date right away.
  • Two years after you agree, we ask again whether you want to keep receiving them. Until then, the server also stops sending to agreements older than two years.
  • Opting out: turn off "Promotional alerts" in the app under Menu → Settings. Sending stops immediately and the device token is deleted from the server. You can also turn off DogPuzzle notifications in your device settings; the app setting is then turned off the next time you open the app.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete your data at any time.

Delete in the app (immediate)
  1. Open DogPuzzle.
  2. Tap Menu (☰) at the top right.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete My Ranking Data.
  5. Confirm, and your data is deleted immediately.

If your app is set to another language, the same items appear in that language.

Request by email

If you have already deleted the app, email support@koinoroom.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the nickname you used. We will delete your data and reply within 7 business days.

6. Security measures

  • All communication between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Server data has row-level access control: users can change only their own information, and administrative functions are limited to registered administrators who have completed two-step verification.
  • Secrets such as keys for sending notifications are not put in the app or source code; they are kept only in the server's secret store.

7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. For residents of the Republic of Korea, children under 14 may not use the app without the consent of a legal guardian. If we learn that information from a child under 14 has been collected, we delete it without delay. Please contact us at the address below with any questions.

8. Sale of personal information

The developer does not sell the information it handles to third parties.
